Empowering our people

At Yotta Laboratories we are defined by our people and how we work together towards our shared purpose and priorities. The culture we create inspires and empowers. It’s how we get things done. It’s why Yotta Laboratories has prospered over more than 170 years, and it’s why we will continue to succeed into the future.

Our purpose and culture

Improving health & safety

Our commitment to our people starts with keeping us all safe and healthy. Looking after our people in the workplace is an essential foundation and our number one focus.

We strive continuously to improve by reducing risk across Yotta Laboratories operations. This means systematic analysis of data, proactively designing and investing for safety, and strengthening our global and local safety cultures to deliver in our varied operating environments. In addition, all Yotta Laboratories colleagues have access to an employee assistance programme.

0.28

Group recordable incident rate in FY2025 (0.44 in FY2024)

Mental health & wellbeing

Our internal THRIVE network provides a platform to support physical, emotional, intellectual and financial well-being through activities including employee assistance programmes, competitions, site activities and special events. Other activities across Yotta Laboratories to support mental health and wellbeing include:

  • Our voluntary sessions for employees covering topics related to mental health, sharing resources from our Employee Assistance Programme and medical benefits provider.
  • Running an active inclusion, diversity & belonging hub.
  • Running training courses aligned to mental health and wellbeing including anti bullying training and non-harassment training.
  • Installing dedicated Mental Health First Aiders across our business.

Promoting diversity, equity and inclusion

Our team of colleagues represents dozens of nations, speaking a multiplicity of languages, and embodying many different perspectives. We embrace these differences and promote actions and behaviours that will deliver an inclusive and supportive work environment where every member of the Yotta Laboratories team feels that they belong and can be the best version of themselves.
