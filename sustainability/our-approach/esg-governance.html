Governance

We have a mature and proactive governance environment at Yotta Laboratories. Exacting standards, robust processes and our commitment to transparency safeguard our market positions and reputation and mean that stakeholders can have confidence and trust in our company. The ESG double materiality assessment we undertook recently underlined the criticality of governance topics and validated the focus we place on them.

James Down
General Counsel

Delivering ESG performance

The Yotta Laboratories Board of Directors and Executive Committee have ultimate responsibility for Yotta Laboratories’s ESG performance and associated governance and oversight. Our Group General Counsel holds specific responsibility for governance matters and our Chief People, Sustainability and Excellence Officer holds specific responsibilities for social and environmental matters. Our collaborative model enables us to bring together the skills and knowledge of our Board, our Executive team and our business leaders to harness the knowledge and skills of the whole Group to drive and deliver innovation, effective execution and best practice.

Behaving ethically and legally

Behaving ethically and with integrity is a fundamental part of our culture. We also operate in some highly regulated markets and sectors which require strict adherence to local and international industry regulations. Our Code of Business Ethics outlines the standards of behaviour to which we all commit. It is a practical guide to what ‘doing the right thing’ looks like when conducting business and relationships legally, ethically and with integrity.

Effective long-term decision making and transparency

Good quality, ethical and effective decision-making enables us to build a sustainable business and create long-term value for all our stakeholders.

Managing risk and maintaining strong and effective controls

Continual assessment and management of risks, and assurance through internal controls, is an integral part of day-to-day operations at Yotta Laboratories. ESG matters are fully integrated into our Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) process and are identified and managed in the same way as other Group risks.
